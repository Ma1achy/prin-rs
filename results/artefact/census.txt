HOW TO READ THIS

**`scalefree` first.** If any row reads BROKEN the census is measuring magnitude
and every number above it is void.

**Then the AZ row on `config_stability` against `preset_prho`.** If they do not
separate, the metric has no subject and the integrator columns say nothing.

**`lgst/shrp` is not enough either, and `plain_rk4` is why.** It has NO reference
body and therefore no wedge mechanism, and it still scores a large connected
component -- a wrecked field is connected too. Density and connectedness cannot
tell a decision boundary from damage.

**`str_lgst` and `str_med` are the discriminator.** 0.0 is a perfect line, 1.0 is
isotropic; `spatial::straightness` is total least squares in closed form, and
`tests/straightness.rs` shows it scores a line 0.000000 against a wandering curve
0.367927 at MATCHED extent and count, and is rotation-invariant. A decision
boundary is STRAIGHT; a fractal boundary and a damage blob are not.

**`ncomp` guards the median.** It counts components of >= 20 px; a short component
is straight by construction, so a median over dust would read as a boundary.
