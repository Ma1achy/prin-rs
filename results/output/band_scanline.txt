HOW TO READ THIS

The banded quantity is the one with a SMALL distinct count whose steps line up with the
8-bit lightness edges. `t_end` is kept in the table as the NEGATIVE CONTROL: it is
genuinely quantised and the render is bitwise identical with and without that
quantisation, so a field can be stepped and irrelevant at the same time.

If `spread_shape` itself has a small distinct count, the arcs are in the ensemble
statistic. If it is smooth and the 8-BIT column steps anyway, the arcs are the display
quantisation of a smooth field -- contours of a gentle gradient at 1/255 intervals,
which is a rendering property and not a physics one.
