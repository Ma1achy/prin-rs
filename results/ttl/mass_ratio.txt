HOW TO READ THIS

**Read the control first, then `nonfin` and `err>10`, then the gain.** A win over an arm
that produced no data is not a win.

**`TTL cost` is part of the result.** A time transformation that shrinks the step where
it should spends more steps to do it; a gain bought at 3x the evaluations is a different
claim from one bought at parity.

**The prediction is that gain rises with q and is zero at q = 1.** A gain that is flat in
q would say the mass weighting is not the mechanism, whatever its sign.
