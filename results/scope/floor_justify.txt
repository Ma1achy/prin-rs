HOW TO READ IT
  If `alpha` ROSE between the arms, the old Floor firings were the integration bug:
  refinement really does reduce the spread on a clean substrate, and a predicate that
  only ever fired on broken data has no subject. If `alpha` is UNCHANGED and only the
  count moved, Floor still measures something and `alpha_lo` is merely mis-placed.
  The sweep is what separates them: a distribution that moved shows Floor firing again
  at a HIGHER `alpha_lo` under `now`; one that did not shows the same curve shifted.
