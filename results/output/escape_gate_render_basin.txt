HOW TO READ THIS

`frozen` is the exposure the patchwork mechanism acts through -- the fraction of pixels
whose shape_vec is read somewhere other than the horizon. If the gate works, it falls,
and the ribbons in `_uniform` become continuous. If `frozen` falls and the arcs stay,
the gate reduced exposure without removing the mechanism, and the next candidates are
the flatness-based escape criterion or not terminating on escape at all.

`config_basin_*_outcome.png` is the CONTROL. In basin mode the colour is the terminal
outcome, so freezing cannot corrupt it; the two implementations should agree exactly and
a disagreement is a SECOND, independent bug.
