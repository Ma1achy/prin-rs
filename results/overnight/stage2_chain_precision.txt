HOW TO READ THIS

**The f64 row is the control.** Chain is a round-off fix; if it moves f64 by more than a
fraction of a decade it is doing something other than preserving digits, and the f32
number cannot then be attributed to precision.

**A null at f32 is a real answer.** It would say the `far` inversion measured in Stage 0
is not a separation-differencing effect, and that the mechanism behind it is still
unidentified -- which is where this investigation already stands, since the conditioning
story proposed for `far` was refuted by its own prediction.
