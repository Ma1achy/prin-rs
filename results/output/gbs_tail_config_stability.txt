HOW TO READ THIS

**A lift needs its base rate above it.** A predictor firing on every pixel scores 1.000 
by arithmetic and one firing on none scores NaN; both are labelled rather than quoted.

**`hot with NO counter set` is the number the comparison turns on.** Budget exhaustion 
truncates a trajectory and an overshoot corrupts one; neither is GBS being inaccurate. 
What is left over is.
