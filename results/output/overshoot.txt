== 1. THE FIGURE-EIGHT -- CONVERGENCE ORDER ==
Chenciner-Montgomery at equal masses, integrated over exactly one period
(T = 6.32591398), n_sync = 32 fixed so the number of boundaries -- and so the
number of overshoots -- is the same at every `eta`. `closure` is the max component
difference between the state at T and the state at 0.

**READ THE ORDER, NOT THE ERROR.** An error falls for many reasons; only the order says
the leading term changed. Without the clamp the overshoot is O(h) and dominates an RK4
march; with it, the boundary contributes at the stepper's own order.

== 4. IS THIS SLICE RESOLVED AT ALL? ==
Arm D at eta, eta/2, eta/4. **If the image keeps moving as the step falls, no single
bug is the answer** -- the slice is unresolved at these settings and every render from
it is a picture of the discretisation as much as of the physics. Horizon 50 is close to
the f64 predictability horizon for this system, so this is a live possibility rather
than a formality. The figure to watch is whether `chord p50` FALLS between the rungs.
