A row with 1/64 distinct and every quad collapsed is the failure: the tree is not
small because the region is tame, it is small because there is nothing in it. Read
`spread(collap)` beside it — that is the number the criterion saw, and at ~1e-17 it
is twelve orders below any tau anyone would set. Nothing downstream can tell that
apart from a perfectly resolved quad.

READ `budget?` AND THE STOP REASONS BEFORE THE COUNTS. At a bound budget `quads`
and `leaves` are arithmetic in the split count and not evidence: 99 splits give
1 + 4*99 = 397 computed and 397 - 99 = 298 leaves, whatever the tree did. Depth 0
is bound here, so only `depth` and the stop reasons carry information in it. And
28 of the 32 rows are inert as a live arm -- every block from depth 14 down reads
21 quads / 16 leaves / depth 2 for all four paths, camera-vetoed. The `distinct`
column is the arm with teeth, and it is an IDENTITY: no integration enters it.

`root decision` explains the one-quad rows: at depth 40 the root quad's own cell
width is already below the PRECISION floor (level ~36 at half0 = 0.05), so the
descent stops for a numerical reason before any decode path is tested. That is a
different limit from the collapse, and the column keeps them apart.
