r_coll is a threshold on a monotone quantity, so raising it can only turn
non-collisions into collisions. The table shows how fast - and the answer is
that on this slice there is NO PLATEAU to sit on. The collision fraction goes
0.0000 -> 0.0242 -> 1.0000 across three decades, because every pixel's d_min
lands inside a single decade (see 1b). No choice of r_coll in that range is a
physical event threshold; it is a readout of the d_min distribution. d_min_true
is the primary quantity here and the collision label is derived from it.

Note also that `triples` is 0.0000 at every threshold: no pixel on this slice
ever has two pairs below r_coll at once, so the >=2-pair rule is exercised only
by construction in tests/outcome_encoding.rs, never by this data.
