== 1. THE MEASURED GAP, and the tau it implies ==
Closure at the FINAL boundary of a run with **nothing terminal**, so every
trajectory reaches the same playhead. With `stop_on_event` on, a collided run freezes
and its shape stops changing -- closure reads exactly 0, which lands in the BOUND
population and destroys the gap it is supposed to measure. Collided trajectories are
split out and shown, not folded in: collision has its own arm and fires first.

`sep` is p50(bound)/p50(esc) -- above 1 is the reference's picture. `tau*` is the
geometric midpoint of p99(esc) and p1(bound). |dn/dt| ~ 1/t^3, so the gap is a
function of MATURITY: the reference quotes 383x at t = 25-30 and this ships at 13.

HOW TO READ THIS
  §0 decides whether the window is safe before any distribution is read. A case whose
     `t_close` is at or below `w(k=1)` can alias an inner orbit into reading settled.
  §1 sets tau. If `tau*` is stable across regions a single value works; if the gap is
     negative the populations overlap and NO fixed tau separates them there.
  §2 recall < 1 is the right failure direction; precision < 1 is not -- BUT read it
     against §3's `u end`. The ground truth demands 3x growth by 3*t_max, so a slow
     genuine escape fails to be certified, and a `precision` shortfall with `u end`
     at 1.0000 is the GROUND TRUTH missing them, not the criterion inventing them.
  §3 IS THE ONE THAT DECIDES. Near 1.0 means nothing re-binds and stopping is safe.
     Near 0 means the criterion is latching transients and it is NOT the fix.
  §4 says whether the replay bought anything. `at entry` near 1 means it did not.
  §5 `shape d` near 0 is the prediction: freezing a converged trajectory is a no-op.
