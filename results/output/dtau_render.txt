1024^2, one sample per pixel. Only `dtau_mode` differs between rows; every other
setting is `closure_render`'s, which is what the committed "before" images used.

`nonfin` is the magenta count -- pixels with a non-finite copy. `simfail` is the
budget-exhausted count, SEPARATE on purpose: a fix that traded one for the other would
not have fixed anything. `hot` is the fraction above 1e-6 energy drift -- the speckle.
`ramp` is each panel's own auto-range window, and it belongs with the image: a clean
field and a blown-up one both fill the ramp, so the window is where the magnitude is.

HOW TO READ THIS

THE PREDICTION, stated so it can fail: the magenta clusters shrink sharply or vanish,
the speckle halos around them go with them and the outcome bands become SOLID as in the
reference GLSL, the continuous ribbons stop being interrupted, and nothing new appears.

`nonfin` and `hot` are the numbers behind the first two clauses. `outcome labels
flipped` is the number behind the third: the speckle IS label flipping, so if the
bands are solidifying that count is large and concentrated where the halos were.

If the magenta survives, that is a SECOND cause and it is worth knowing before
anything is built on top of this one.
