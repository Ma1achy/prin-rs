HOW TO READ THIS

**`land resid` is the quantity, `closure` is the consequence.** The residual falls nine
orders to round-off; the closure then falls until it meets the FIXTURE's own ~4.10e-8
floor, which the corrected rows sit on at every step size. **No order may be read off a
corrected row** -- that flat 4.1022e-8 is nine-significant-digit initial conditions, not
a method.

**KDK is the control.** Second order, so an O(h^2) landing was never its constraint, and
the correction buys it nothing -- it is marginally worse, paying for corrections it does
not need. A fix that improved every arm would not be removing an order-two cap.
