HOW TO READ THIS

**Read the saturation guard first.** If either precision reads DEAD or SATURATED, the
gain line beneath it for that precision is a difference between two numbers that are not
measuring anything, and it does not adjudicate the conditioning question.

**The decision, if the f32 row is LIVE:** an AZ advantage that GROWS at f32 supports
conditioning and justifies Stage 2 (chain coordinates). One that SHRINKS says the
mechanism is not precision, and chain is unlikely to fix `far` either.

**`err>10` is the project's own flag for *this pixel is not data*.** An f32 arm carrying
it on a large share of pixels is not a weaker measurement, it is a different one.
