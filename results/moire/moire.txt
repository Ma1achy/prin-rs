HOW TO READ THIS

**`promin` first.** A peak with prominence near 1 is the largest bin of a FLAT
spectrum, not a band. `lambda` means nothing without it.

**Then `steps p50` across the two `cadence` rows.** If it is not roughly flat the
step size was not held and those rows measure the stepper, not the cadence --
`dtau = eta*dt_left/(A0*B0)` is why scaling eta with n_sync is required at all.

**`t_end dst` and `on bnd` are the standing cadence diagnostic.** Escape is sampled
only at sync boundaries, so where it terminates `t_end` takes n_sync values and
every derived field draws those steps. Read the DELTA across cadences, never the
level: `near-field` reads 97.85% on-boundary while being completely clean, because
its footprints sit at t_end = t_max, and the horizon is a boundary time.
