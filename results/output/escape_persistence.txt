HOW TO READ THIS

[persistence] decides what the stride MEANS. `escape_candidate` is relative energy > 0
and receding, and during a close encounter that can be transiently true. If a material
fraction RE-BIND, a finer stride is not resolving a quantity -- it is latching
transients as terminal events, and a persistence guard is required before the escape
fraction at stride 1 can be believed. If essentially all persist, the stride is a pure
resolution knob and 0.5494 stands.

[precedence] bounds the ordering bug. Under `stop_on_event` the loop breaks on the
first DETECTED event so only one is ever recorded, which is why repairing `classify`
moved one footprint of 5440 in production settings. The reference path accumulates
both, and that count is the size of the defect the repair addresses.
