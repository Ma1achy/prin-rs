HOW TO READ THIS

**`ref chord` is the guard.** It is the distance between the reference and a second
reference at half its step. If it is not far below `chord off`, the reference is not
converged and the row is measuring reference error rather than the arms.

**`gain` is `off / on`.** Above 1 the correction helps the trajectory. The figure-eight
says 1960x for RK4; if a field says ~1, the correction is a periodic-orbit result and
does not transfer, and the case for porting it to AZ and Heggie fails on the evidence
rather than on a blind instrument.

**KDK remains the control**: second order, never limited by an O(h^2) landing, so its
gain must stay near 1 whatever the others do.
