dtau_step -- 48x48 nominal trajectories per case, eta=0.01, max_steps=30000

MODES: `fixed` is the shipped-until-now sizing and what EVERY committed Rust and
NumPy number was taken under. `per-step-rem` is the obvious repair and is Zeno by
arithmetic: dt ~ eta*rem gives rem_(n+1) = rem_n (1-eta), so the interval is
approached geometrically and never completed. `per-step-int` holds dt_left fixed and
recomputes only A*B, capped at the entry value.

== 1. STEP-COUNT DISTRIBUTION -- THE GATE ==
The fix trades accuracy for step count. If the budget is exhausted elsewhere, the
non-finite pixels have been swapped for budget-exhausted ones -- a different failure
wearing a different colour. `budget` is that count and it is not folded into §2.

**`t/t_max` IS THE DISCRIMINATOR AND IT BELONGS HERE, NOT IN §2.** A mode that stalls
has a beautiful drift -- it barely moved. Reading §2 without this column would score
`per-step-rem` as five orders better than either real mode. A difference can be small
because both sides are right or because one side is dead.

== 3. THE SPATIAL TEST -- what ties the numbers to the image ==
`clust` is the fraction of hot pixels with a hot 4-neighbour divided by the same
under a random field of the same density. **1.0 is chance.** `nf w/ hot nbr` is the
fraction of non-finite pixels having a high-drift neighbour -- magenta cores inside
speckled halos. The user measured 1.7x and 6/6 under `fixed`.

Chance is `1 - (1-base)^4` for base density `n_hot/N^2`, so **the ratio is not
comparable across densities** -- it rises simply because the fix thins the mask.
Read `n_hot` as the measurement and the ratio as a shape statistic. And where the
mask SATURATES (deep interior, 92% hot) chance is ~1 and the ratio says nothing:
that is the standing regional mask-saturation result, at a third statistic.

handful of trajectories do worse under the fix; averaging that away is how a second
mechanism stays hidden. The worst regressions are named with the quantities that
would explain them.

denominator, not a bad outcome: near-field's largest ratio is a pixel going

== 5. T::TINY -- IS THE FLOOR EVER THE GUARD? ==
`dtau` divides by `A*B`, floored at TINY (1e-300 at f64, 1e-37 at f32). At f32
`TINY*TINY` UNDERFLOWS, so a doubly-degenerate state gives `dtau = inf` -- caught by
the explicit `is_finite` test and NOT by the floor it is named for. `ab_min` is the
raw product before the floor, so `floored` says whether the clamp ever bound.
