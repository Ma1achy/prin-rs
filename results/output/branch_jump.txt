HOW TO READ THIS

**RATIO is the finding.** Near 1 means crossing the selector's surface costs no more
than an ordinary step, and the argmax is a SYMPTOM -- the trajectories had already
diverged and the geometry followed. Far above 1 means it is a SEED: a jump the dynamics
then amplifies over the remaining horizon.

Read it against the control row, never alone. And `dchart` alone is unreadable: a large
number on a violent interval and a small one on a quiet interval say the same thing,
which is why the local step error is the normaliser.

**And read the confound line before the ratio.** If the time-mismatch displacement is a
material fraction of `dr_chart`, the arms were compared at different physical times and
the difference is partly the time transformations rather than the charts. `raw/corrected`
near 1 means the correction changed nothing and the comparison was clean anyway.
