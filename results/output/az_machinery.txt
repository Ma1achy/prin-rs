HOW TO READ THIS

**`steps p50` is the control and must be roughly flat across the CONTROLLED rows.** If
it moves, `eta` did not hold the step size and the comparison is a step-size comparison
wearing a re-registration label.

`hot lift` is against the baseline's hot set: 1.0 is chance, 4.0 is perfect agreement.
**A high lift means the wedges survive the change; a lift near 1 means they moved.**
`chord p50` is the median |delta log10 drift|, the magnitude.

The `confounded` row is what the uncontrolled experiment would have claimed. Compare it
against `n=250 CONTROLLED`: if they differ, the control was load-bearing.
