HOW TO READ THIS

THE PREDICTION, stated so it can fail: the stacked-crescent banding in the green and
blue mid-field of `_uniform` is PRESENT in arm B and GONE in arm D; arm C is smooth too
and probably smoother than A; the white regions do not grow further and ideally shrink
back toward A; magenta stays at arm B's level, near zero, because that was the `dtau`
fix and it stands; and drift improves in D over B, not merely over A.

If the crescents survive in arm D, that is a THIRD mechanism and it is worth knowing
immediately rather than explaining.

MEASURED, AND THE FIRST CLAUSE FAILED. The crescents are present in ALL FOUR arms,
including A, which predates both changes -- so neither knob draws them. Under
outcome-class colouring arm D's arcs vanish entirely while the region boundaries
sharpen, which is RESULTS §21's standing result at a new site: the banding is a
colouring artefact, the crisp edges are not. Every other clause held, and the magenta
ran 30109 -> 2071 (clamp alone) -> 178 (both). See RESULTS §24.8.

(A->B against C->D) is 2.5x here and 66x on the coarse grid -- the coarse grid is
dominated by the tame majority while a million samples land in the chaotic population.
