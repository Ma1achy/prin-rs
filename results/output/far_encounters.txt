HOW TO READ THIS

**A region named for a large separation is not a region without an encounter.** `far`
names where the SAMPLED body sits. Bodies 1 and 2 hold their Burrau positions on every
pixel of the slice, so their mutual encounter is the same one everywhere -- which is why
`d_min` is both the deepest here and the most uniform.

This matters for every comparison on `far`: an integrator is being graded on a region
whose whole frame contains a near-collision, not on a smooth field. The unregularised
control failing on all of it is then the expected result rather than a surprise, and
logH doing worst there is the collision finding at field scale -- it slows the clock but
does not remove the singularity, so the encounter still has to be RESOLVED.
