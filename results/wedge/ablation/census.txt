HOW TO READ THIS

**`az_prefix` FIRST.** It is AZ with the step-control fixes off -- the kernel the
wedges were first seen under. If it does not carry a large dense component with a
STRAIGHT boundary, this metric cannot see the artefact and every row below it is
void. A null from a metric that never had a subject is this project's most
frequently catalogued failure.

**Then `az` against `heggie`.** `az` is the post-fix kernel, so what it still
carries is the RESIDUAL the step-control work did not remove -- which is what the
re-registration mechanism predicts Heggie should not have.

**The two mask columns are each other's control.** The rendered one is the mask
`circled_ics.rs` defined and is comparable with what was circled; the field one is
`spread_shape` above the shared AZ window's top, which is what saturation means,
with no 8-bit round trip and no chroma term. `jacc` is their agreement. If they
disagree, the render is adding or hiding something and no row should be quoted.

**`plain_rk4` is the no-reference-body control.** It has no chart to re-register
into, so it cannot have the artefact -- but its field is wrecked, which is what
says a low `bnd str` means straight and not merely empty. Read `dense` beside it.
