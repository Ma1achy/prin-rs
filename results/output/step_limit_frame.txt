HOW TO READ THIS

**`overshoot` must be zero.** It counts steps that carried the interval clock past twice
its own interval -- `dt > dt_left` is a bug, not a condition to handle. A nonzero
baseline column is the defect being measured; a nonzero column under a candidate means
that candidate does not address it.

**`err>10` against `secs` is the decision.** A mode that reaches the baseline's error at
the baseline's cost has done nothing; one that reaches ~1.0 for a few percent has won.
`Global` is the control and is expected to pay in proportion to its `eta` multiplier --
if it does not beat the others on cost it should not ship, and if it does, it should.

**The controls decide whether a win generalises.** A mode that only helps
`config_stability` is tuned to it. `preset_shape` must be untouched by all four.
