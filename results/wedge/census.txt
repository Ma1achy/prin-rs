READ AFTER THE FACT -- what this run establishes and what it does not
=====================================================================

THE SUBJECT CONTROL PASSES. `az_prefix` -- AZ with the step-control fixes off, the kernel the
wedges were first seen under -- carries a dense-pale set of 0.0026 of the frame against the
record's 0.0018 at 1024^2 (`results/circled/README.md`: 1935 px of 1048576). Same order. The mask
is reproducing the object that was circled.

AND THE STEP-CONTROL FIXES ARE WHAT REMOVED IT. `az_prefix -> az` takes the dense set
0.0026 -> 0.0001, a 26x reduction, and non-finite 1153 -> 0. That is the `dtau` fix, the landing
clamp and the predictive step limit, all of which predate Heggie.

HEGGIE ADDS NOTHING THIS CENSUS CAN SEE. `az`, `heggie`, `logh_rk4` and `plain_rk4` agree to
three or four digits on every column -- pale 0.0127, dense 0.0001, largest 5, field-dense 0.1524,
boundary straightness 0.588-0.592. **`plain_rk4` has no regularisation at all**, so four
unrelated integrators landing on the same numbers says the remaining signal is not the
integrator's. At `t_max = 50` on a chaotic slice, `spread_shape` saturating on ~15% of the frame
is the physics.

WHAT DID NOT RUN, AND IT IS THE THING THAT WAS ASKED FOR
--------------------------------------------------------

**`bnd str` on the rendered panel is `NaN` on every row.** The dense set is there -- 682 px on
`az_prefix` -- but **fragmented 24x**: its largest connected component is 28 px, below the
40-boundary-point minimum a local fit needs. So the straightness measurement never executed on
the mask that defines the object. The record's fit ran over **621 rows of a 1024^2 panel**; at
512^2 the same structure is shattered into sub-40-px pieces.

**And the two masks disagree.** Jaccard 0.053-0.071. This harness's own instruction is that when
they disagree the render is adding or hiding something and no row should be quoted. The field
column at 15-21% is dominated by genuine chaotic divergence at `t = 50`, not by the artefact --
which is why its `bnd str` sits mid-scale at 0.59, neither the 0.00 of a straight edge nor the
0.72 of a jagged one.

So: **the numbers above support "the step-control fixes removed the wedges and Heggie adds
nothing further", and they do NOT support any statement about edge shape.** The straightness
question needs 1024^2, which is 4x this run.
