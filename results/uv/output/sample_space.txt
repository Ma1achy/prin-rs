THE READING: `QuadLocal` buys NOTHING at any depth in this table, and the reason is
a different global sum one level up.

§12's defect is real -- `jitter` did recover `du` from a `u` the linespace had just
built. Its cost is zero, because `decode::linearise` differences the chart at
`cu +/- half`, which is the SAME global sum, and it collapses first: `|ju|` tracks
`half` exactly to depth 44, quantises to the ulp of `cx` at 48, and is EXACTLY ZERO
at 52. A linearisation whose own secant has collapsed carries no information for a
carried `du` to preserve, so all four f64 cells must fail at the same rung -- and
they do, at 48. The fix §12 actually needs is a Jacobian not built by a secant at
the cell width.

And `g/L32` is the control that stops the f32 result being misread: it is IDENTICAL
to `l/L32` at every rung, so the ~28 levels the linearised f32 path holds over
`g/f32` are `LinSplitF32`'s own -- the standing result, measured under `Global` long
before this flag existed -- and not `QuadLocal`'s. Without that column the table
would have read as this change buying 28 levels.

The ORIGIN ladder is the control for the whole finding: with `cx = 0` nothing
collapses at any rung in any cell and `|ju|` tracks `half` to 4.3e-20, because there
is no O(1) neighbour for the increment to be absorbed into. Quote the coordinate
magnitude with any floor depth.
