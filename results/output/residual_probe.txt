== DOES IT CONVERGE? ==
A triple collision is a genuine singularity AZ does not remove, so `error_ratio` will
NOT fall with `eta`. Ordinary under-resolution will. **This is the whole question**, and
it is the project's own diagnostic signature: a quantity that does not converge under
refinement is measuring the sampling rather than the system.

A STUCK pixel is not a defect. `error_ratio` exists to say *this pixel is not data*, and
a trajectory that passes arbitrarily close to a triple collision is a measurement
outcome, not missing data -- the no-discard rule. A pixel that RESOLVES is ordinary
under-resolution and `f` could be tightened, at a cost the table in
`results/step_control/README.md` prices.
