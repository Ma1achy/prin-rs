13 charts: still + wire twin + outcome control + level ladder (both) + .prnq each,
plus the two gallery APNGs. Everything at 1024^2.

`distinct` is how many distinct values the lightness field takes over the chart. Read it
before the picture: a field with few distinct values has that many colours in it, and no
ramp recovers what is not there.

`ramp span` is hi/lo of the p1-p99 window. The ramp is auto-ranged per chart, so a chart
with no dynamic range has its NOISE stretched to full scale and reads as structure. A
span near 1 means the picture is of the estimator, not the physics.

`screen` is leaves stopped by the screen floor rather than by the criterion. It is a
VETO on scale and never a trigger, and it is view-relative -- a floored quad refines
again when zoomed into.

Leaf counts are slice-conditional to 4.3x and compare WITHIN a chart, never across.
`alpha med` and `alpha idec` are the cross-chart quantities. The interdecile rather than
the variance: excess kurtosis on alpha_shape is 110, so the variance describes the tail.

READ THE `bound` COLUMN BEFORE ANY LEAF COUNT. A row marked BUDGET stopped because the
budget ran out, not because the criterion was satisfied -- its quads, leaves and depth
are facts about the budget and are the same for every such row by construction. Only a
`crit` row's tree shape is a statement about the chart.

And the substantive finding this table carries: the reference's chart families, centred
where they are centred here, are TAME. `alpha med` sits at 0.99-1.01 on every latent,
Burrau and simplex row against 0.14 for `body_plane` and 0.19 for the shape sphere.
alpha near 1 means splitting halves the spread -- refinement pays, so the scheduler
refines everywhere and runs to the budget. That is correct behaviour on a tame region,
not a scheduler fault.

But it means these charts are not exercising the criterion where it is hard. Tameness is
a property of WHERE a chart is centred, and the base latent point here was chosen to
avoid the z = 0 symmetry rather than to find chaos. Moving it until the picture gets
interesting would be tuning; the honest report is that the interesting question -- does
the criterion behave consistently across charts -- is not answered by a set of charts
that are all tame.
