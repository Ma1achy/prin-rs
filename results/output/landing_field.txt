HOW TO READ THIS

**`land resid` is the quantity and `drift` is the consequence.** The figure-eight says
the residual falls nine orders and a better-than-second-order stepper gains ~1960x. This
asks whether that survives a chaotic field with close approaches in it.

**KDK is the control.** Second order, so an O(h^2) landing was never its binding
constraint. If it improves here too, the correction is doing something other than
removing an order-two cap and the figure-eight account is wrong.

**If the RK4 and GBS rows improve materially, that is the case for porting the secant
landing to AZ and Heggie** -- whose measured orders of 2.08 and 2.40 are exactly what an
O(h^2) landing allows. That port would invalidate every committed number in `results/`,
so it is a decision and not a consequence.
