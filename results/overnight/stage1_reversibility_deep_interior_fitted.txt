HOW TO READ THIS

**PREDICTION 1, recorded before the run: reversibility tracks FTLE more strongly than
drift does.** Drift-vs-FTLE is a published null for AZ (-0.0820 against a shifted
-0.1022).

**PREDICTION 1b: `rev/amp` should track FTLE MUCH LESS than `rev` does.** That is the
test of the normalisation itself. If the two columns correlate alike, the division did
not remove the amplification and `rev/amp` is not integrator quality -- it is `rev` under
another name, and must not be reported as the integrator arm.

**Compare arms DOWN a column only.** RK4 is not time-symmetric and KDK is; across
steppers this statistic scores symmetry rather than accuracy.
