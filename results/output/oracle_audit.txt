HOW TO READ THIS

`dp_optimal` is the exact minimum over ALL tree-shaped leaf sets at each budget. It is a
BOUND: no row may sit below it. A row that does is a harness bug, and every error(B)
number in the corpus is suspect until it is found.

`greedy_lookahead_1` is greedy on immediate delta-error. It is a reference and NOT a
bound -- it has been measured below the random band on `far`. Read the three roles from
the header: floor, reference, ceiling.

The `distinct` column decides what a flat row means. A flat curve on thousands of
distinct values is an ordering that is actively bad; a flat curve on one distinct value
is no ordering at all, and the row is the tie-break's scan order. They are different
faults with different fixes and error(B) alone cannot tell them apart.

On a SMOOTH field the two converge on the same answer: a quad's spread over a gradient
is proportional to its cell width, so argmax-on-spread IS breadth-first, and a constant
signal falls through to a level-first tie-break which is also breadth-first. That is why
`far`'s rows agree to five digits across every budget. A criterion can only differ from
breadth-first where the field is not smooth -- and smoothness is exactly where there is
nothing to find. `far` degenerating is correct behaviour, not a defect.
