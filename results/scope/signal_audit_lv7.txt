HOW TO READ THIS

[1] is a MEASUREMENT AND A STOP. If p99/p1 sits near 1.0 at every level, cost-aware
priority is arithmetically incapable of reordering anything at this configuration and
the cost axis is written off with the numbers rather than argued about. `far` being
exactly constant is the mechanism showing: smooth region, no encounters, every
trajectory the same work.

[2] conditions everything after it. If the optimum's label for a quad FLIPS between
budgets, then no budget-independent signal can be optimal at both, and the per-level
`captured` result is a symptom of that rather than of a bad signal. Read the shared
count before the churn.

[3] separates `does not know` from `knows but is drowned out`. Spearman is scale-free;
error(B) is operational. A signal high on the first and poor on the second is being
wasted by the aggregation, which is exactly the failure that excluded diffusion.

[4] says whether the signals are REDUNDANT. Read the held-out columns against the
in-sample one: 55 features on tens of thousands of quads will fit near-perfectly and
prove nothing. And three heterogeneous regions is NOT a validation set -- the regions
differ in kind, so the folds are never averaged and a null from them is weak evidence
either way.
