HOW TO READ IT
  ONE distinct leaf set at a tau below the bulk = the standing result survives, `far`
  is still the control, and the tail does not reach the descent. MORE than one = the
  tail is doing work, `far` orders criteria differently now, and every result leaning
  on "thirteen criteria give the identical leaf set" needs re-reading.
  At tau=1e-4 the answer is one set FOR THE WRONG REASON -- 16 leaves, all `keep`,
  nothing ever ranked. That row is the degeneracy control, not a result.
