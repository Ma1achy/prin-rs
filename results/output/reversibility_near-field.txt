  **`disp` is the guard.** It is the forward displacement in the same units. A row whose

  **The `eta` ladder is the discriminator.** Falling with `eta` is truncation and the

  it off. One `eta` cannot tell those apart.

HOW TO READ THIS

**A saturating column is the expected result on a chaotic region, not a failure.** It
says the residual is round-off amplified by the flow, which is a property of the field.
A column that falls with `eta` is one where truncation still dominates and the
integrator has room.

**`rev/disp` near 1 means the trajectory has lost its way entirely** -- the return is as
far from the start as the forward leg went. Near 0 means the march retraced.

**What this cannot do is rank steppers.** Time-symmetry is worth orders here and is not
accuracy. The comparison this supports is regularisation at fixed stepper: `az` against
`heggie` against `logh_rk4`, all three RK4.
