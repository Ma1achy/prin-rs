The coupling question: does the ORDERING of criteria change between colourings?
Absolute errors are NOT comparable across colourings -- each has its own reference and
its own zero. Only the ranking within a block is.

If the ordering is stable, the criterion is not sensitive to the lightness field and
§6's concern does not bite in practice. If it reorders -- particularly if a criterion
that wins under `outcome` loses under `diffusion` -- then the criterion needs a term
for the lightness field and currently has none.

The FTLE march is UNREGULARISED (tb.py's fixed-step leapfrog, the pair tb_ftle.py has
a reference for). Near a close approach it is not trustworthy, so an FTLE-lightness
result in `deep interior` carries that caveat and a spread-lightness one does not.
