HOW TO READ THIS

`frozen` is the exposure the patchwork acts through. It does NOT have to be small:
under this criterion freezing a converged trajectory is close to a no-op, so a high
`frozen` with a `shape d` near zero is the *expected* result, not a warning.

`shape d` decides. Median AND max AND the moved count, because an aggregate can only
say the distribution did not move -- twice on this project a row identical to five
digits hid every pixel moving, worst 6.7% and 1.86x.

`config_basin_*_outcome.png` is the CONTROL. In basin mode the colour IS the terminal
outcome, so freezing cannot corrupt it and the two toggle rows must agree exactly.
A disagreement there is a SECOND fault, in the physics or the event detection, and
it needs its own investigation rather than being folded into this one.
