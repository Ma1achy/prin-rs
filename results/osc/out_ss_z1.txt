SUPERSAMPLE at zf = 0.06, output 192^2. `f` is samples per output pixel edge.
The colour window is taken ONCE at f = 1 and shared -- a per-factor window would
rescale each panel and the amplitudes would stop being comparable.

  f = 1  copies kept on 36864 of 36864 px;  plain != resolved on 1878 px  -> NOT INERT
  f = 1   marched 192^2 = 36864 pixels

`plain` isolates the FIELD sampling; `ssres` adds the shipped hue supersampler on
top. If the cross-hatch falls with `f` in `plain` it is an alias of real structure.
If `ssres` moves it and `plain` does not, it was the hue channel; the lightness
channel has no per-copy analogue and `rgb_resolved` cannot reach it.
