HOW TO READ THIS

[1] is the test the fix stands or falls on. Ungated, the escapes that only a fine
stride sees do not persist -- 0 of 895 in `deep interior`. If the gated rows do not
read near 1.000 at +1 through +8, the distance gate is NOT the fix and no render
should be quoted from it.

[2] the escape fraction is expected to FALL where transients were being latched
(`deep interior`) and to be essentially UNCHANGED where escape genuinely terminates
(`preset_plambda`). A guard that cuts both is cutting too much, and that arm is what
separates a guard from a refusal.

[4] `r_esc` is a new constant. The curve is the argument for whatever value ships, and
a plateau in it is what says the answer is not sensitive to the choice.

The `bodies` column is the SECOND divergence from the numpy form and it is not free.
`one` is what this port has shipped; `all` is what the GLSL does. Read the two rows at
`r_esc = 0` first -- their difference is the body arm on its own, with no gate.
