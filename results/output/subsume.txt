HOW TO READ THIS

The row that decides is **`FixedPerInterval` + `Predictive`** against
**`PerStepInterval` + `Predictive`**. If they agree, the cap is redundant once the
per-step limit is in force and one of the three controllers can be deleted. If the
`Fixed` row is worse, the cap is doing work the limit does not, and both stay --
which is a real answer and not a failure of the experiment.

`FixedPerInterval` + `None` is the historical behaviour and is here as the fourth cell,
because a 2x2 read from three cells is an inference.
