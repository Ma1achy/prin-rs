`seed move` is the p90 of |alpha(seed k) - alpha(seed 0)| over the same quads: sampling
noise on the real signal, everything else fixed. Compare it against `sib p90`.

If seed move ~ sib p90, the sibling range is measuring its own draw and the Sibling
policy is thresholding on sampling error. If seed move << sib p90, the siblings
genuinely disagree and the statistic carries signal -- though `alpha` is a chaotic
quantity, so part of any residual is divergence that no ensemble size reduces.
