HOW TO READ THIS

**`_drift` is the panel to read for a numerical question, not `_uniform`.** The science
fields show a defect only after it has propagated into a spread or a label; the drift
map shows it at source, as coherent structure with the non-finite pixels inside it.

**`_outcome` is the panel that separates a colouring artefact from real structure.**
Arcs that vanish under the discrete map and edges that survive and sharpen are the
standing signature: the banding is a colouring artefact, the crisp edges are not.

**`chord max` of 2.000 is antipodal and is NOT evidence of anything.** Two correct
integrators through a chaotic region must diverge over t = 13 or 50. These images answer
whether the structure got cleaner. They do not answer whether the pixels agree, and no
chord between the two integrators can.
