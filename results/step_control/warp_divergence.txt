HOW TO READ THIS

**The absolute divergence factor is the FIELD's, not the mode's.** Step counts vary
lane to lane with or without retries, so the control row is large too. Read each
candidate's factor against the control's (1.577 linear, 1.432 tiled): the INCREASE is
what reject-and-retry costs a warp.

`warps hit` is the fraction of warps containing at least one retrying lane. It is the
other half of the picture: rare retries that are perfectly scattered stall every warp,
and frequent retries that cluster stall few. A low retry rate with a high `warps hit` is
the bad case, and it is invisible in any per-pixel average.
