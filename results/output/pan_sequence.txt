Zero payload mismatches is the property a cache would need: a recomputed reduction
and a cached one are interchangeable. It holds because the ensemble offsets are a
fixed Halton prefix indexed by COPY -- not by pixel, not by camera, not by time --
so a quad's ensemble does not know the camera exists.

`recompute%` is 100 by construction: nothing is cached, so everything is recomputed
every frame. `would-cache%` is what a cache would have served, and is the number a
caching design would be buying. No eviction policy is implemented and none is implied.

`in view` uses Camera::covers, which the scheduler never consults. If it ever does,
that is view culling and a scope change -- the screen floor is a veto on SCALE, and
adding a position term to it would make a quad's decision depend on where the camera
is pointing, which is the thing `never cached as a quad fact` exists to keep out.
