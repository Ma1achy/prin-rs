HOW TO READ IT

  so every criterion comparison in the corpus is largely intact and the numbers are not.
  `rho` far from 1.0 = the field was re-ordered and the criterion work is a full
  re-derivation. `antip` near 1.0 means the chord column is SATURATED and says nothing:
