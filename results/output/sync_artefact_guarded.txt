HOW TO READ THIS

`t_end distinct` is the decisive number, and `ON A SYNC BOUNDARY` is the mechanism.
A quantised t_end does not merely take few values -- its values ARE the boundary
times, so a high on-boundary fraction at escape_every=0 that collapses at the fine
cadence is the artefact, demonstrated rather than inferred.

`frac_hot_between` is printed WITH ITS CEILING because it is a fraction over N^2
footprints and cannot exceed N^2+1 distinct values however fine the cadence gets.
Reading a saturation there as evidence about the physics is reading the resolution
of a 64-sample fraction.

`escape` vs `collision` bounds the whole effect before any count is read: only an
escape-terminated footprint can carry a boundary time, so a region terminating by
collision cannot be contaminated by this at all -- whatever its images look like.

`d_min_true` by terminal state decides whether the finer cadence is a FIX or a BUG.
`escape_candidate` asks whether a body is unbound and receding, and the reference asks
it only at boundaries, where the state is Cartesian and shared. Asked every RK4 step it
is also asked DURING a close encounter, where a pair's instantaneous two-body energy can
read positive transiently. If the escaped population's d_min collapses toward the
collided population's as the stride tightens, the test is firing mid-encounter and the
finer cadence is wrong rather than finer.
